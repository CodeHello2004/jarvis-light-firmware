Before installing

1

Disconnect AC power

Connect only the low-voltage controller to the computer.

2

Use a USB data cable

A charging-only cable cannot flash the board.

3

Select the COM port

Choose the port belonging to the XIAO.

4

Wait for 100%

Do not unplug or reset during installation.

Safety: Never connect exposed mains wiring while the XIAO is connected to a computer by USB.
